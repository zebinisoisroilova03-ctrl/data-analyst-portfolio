Sales Commission Calculator
📌 Project Overview
This is an independent Excel project created to practice and apply fundamental Excel skills learned during my Data Analyst learning journey.
The project simulates a sales commission system, calculating employee commission, performance-based bonuses, and total payout based on sales amount.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Calculate commission based on sales amount and a fixed commission rate
* Apply a conditional bonus for employees who exceed a sales threshold
* Calculate total payout per employee
* Analyze sales performance using basic Excel functions

🛠️ Excel Skills Used

* Rows and Columns
* Cell References
* Relative References
* Absolute References (`$`)
* Basic Formatting
* `SUM`
* `AVERAGE`
* `MIN`
* `MAX`
* `IF`
* Percentage calculations
* Commission calculations

📊 Commission Rules

* Commission rate: 5% of sales amount
* Bonus threshold: $5,000
* Employees with sales amount above $5,000 receive a bonus of $200
* Total Payout = Commission + Bonus

📈 Summary Analysis
The spreadsheet includes:

* Top performer (maximum sales)
* Lowest sales
* Average sales
* Total sales
* Total commission
* Total payout

📁 File

* `sales-commission-calculator.xlsx`

💡 What I Learned
Through this project, I practiced applying the same core Excel formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, IF, absolute references) to a different business scenario than payroll, and practiced checking my formulas for logical errors (for example, correcting Total Payout to reflect only Commission + Bonus rather than the full sales amount).
